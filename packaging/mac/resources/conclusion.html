Anbu is installed

Open your music app and rescan plugins, or restart it. Anbu is listed under R4N.

  • Logic Pro / GarageBand: restart the app. If Anbu doesn't show up, Logic Pro → Settings → Plug-in Manager → select Anbu → Reset & Rescan Selection.
  • FL Studio: Options → Manage plugins → Find installed plugins.
  • Ableton Live: Settings → Plug-Ins → Rescan (with Use VST3 Plug-In System Folders on).

Files: /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/Anbu.vst3 and /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components/Anbu.component
